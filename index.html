2. Bốn tiêu chí chấm – giám khảo hỏi gì?

Task Fulfillment~50%

Đáp ứng đề

  • Trả lời đủ mọi ý đề hỏi?
  • Đúng thể loại (email ≠ luận), đủ số từ?
  • Mỗi ý có lý do / ví dụ?

→ Gạch chân từng yêu cầu; mỗi yêu cầu = 1 đoạn.

Organization~10%

Bố cục & liên kết

  • Có mở – thân – kết?
  • Mỗi đoạn một ý chính?
  • Từ nối tự nhiên, không lặp?

→ 4 đoạn, 6–8 từ nối khác nhau.

Vocabulary~15%

Từ vựng

  • Đủ từ cho chủ đề?
  • Đúng ngữ cảnh, đúng giọng (formal/informal)?
  • Ít lặp, ít lỗi chính tả?

→ Paraphrase đề; dùng collocation quen tay.

Grammar~25%

Ngữ pháp

  • Đa dạng câu: đơn – ghép – phức?
  • Đúng thì, hoà hợp chủ ngữ – động từ?
  • Lỗi không cản trở người đọc?

→ Mỗi đoạn 1–2 câu phức "ăn chắc", còn lại câu vừa sức.

Tỉ trọng % là ước lượng phổ biến trong tài liệu luyện thi; đề thi kết thúc học phần chấm theo đúng 4 tiêu chí này trên thang 10 (Task 1) và 20 (Task 2).

1. Đề thi trông thế nào

Mẫu đề

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. thời gian gợi ý

You have received an email from your English-speaking friend, Anna, who is planning to visit your city next month. Read part of her email: tình huống → người nhận là ai?

“I'm so excited about my trip! Could you help me with a few things? What's the weather like in your city in November? Which places do you think I should visit? And where would be a good, not too expensive place to stay?”

Write an email of reply to Anna, and: 3 ý bắt buộc

  • tell her about the weather in November,
  • suggest two places she should visit, and
  • recommend a place to stay.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses. ≥120 từ · không ghi tên

Đọc đề
  • Người nhận quyết định giọng: bạn bè → informal; thầy cô, quản lý, công ty, người lạ → formal.
  • 3 gạch đầu dòng = 3 đoạn thân bài. Có thể là dạng formal "góp ý – phản hồi" (what you found most useful · one thing to improve · would you attend again) – vẫn là 3 ý.
  • Không ghi tên, địa chỉ: dòng ký chỉ là Best wishes, / Yours sincerely,.

6. Bảy dạng đề thường gặp – template + bài mẫu

Cụm in đậm = cụm dùng đi dùng lại, đổi nội dung là dùng được cho đề khác. Số từ được đếm tự động.

Dạng A · Trả lời bạn – cung cấp thông tin & gợi ý (informal)

You have received an email from your English-speaking friend, Anna, who is planning to visit your city next month. Read part of her email:

“I'm so excited about my trip! Could you help me with a few things? What's the weather like in your city in November? Which places do you think I should visit? And where would be a good, not too expensive place to stay?”

Write an email of reply to Anna, and:

  • tell her about the weather in November,
  • suggest two places she should visit, and
  • recommend a place to stay.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu A · informal… từ

Dear Anna,

Thanks for your email. It was great to hear that you are coming to my city next month. Here is some information that I hope will help.

First of all, the weather in November is quite pleasant. It is usually cool and dry, so you will not need heavy clothes, but I suggest bringing a light jacket for the evenings.

As for places to visit, you should definitely see the old town, where you can try local street food, and the Central Museum, which shows the history of the region.

Finally, I'd recommend staying at the Riverside Guesthouse. It is clean, friendly and only ten minutes from my house, and a room costs about twenty dollars a night.

Let me know when you arrive so that I can pick you up at the airport. I can't wait to see you!

Best wishes,

Vì sao ăn điểm: đủ 3 ý, mỗi ý = trả lời + chi tiết (cool and dry · twenty dollars a night) + lý do; giọng thân mật nhất quán (I'd, can't, Best wishes); 2 mệnh đề quan hệ (where…, which…) đủ để lên B2.
Dạng B · Phản hồi – góp ý (formal)

You recently attended a two-day workshop on communication skills for health workers, organised by the training centre CareTalk. After the event, you received this email from the coordinator, Mr Lewis:

“Thank you for taking part in our workshop. To help us improve, could you tell us which part of the workshop you found most useful, mention one thing we could do better, and let us know whether you would join another CareTalk event in the future?”

Write an email of reply to Mr Lewis, and:

  • tell him which part you found most useful,
  • suggest one thing that could be improved, and
  • say whether you would join another event and why.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu B · formal… từ

Dear Mr Lewis,

Thank you for your email. I am writing to give you my feedback on the communication skills workshop, which I attended last week.

The part I found most useful was the role-play session on the second day. Practising difficult conversations with "patients" helped me feel more confident, and the trainer's comments were clear and practical.

However, one thing that could be improved is the timetable. The morning lectures were rather long, and there was little time for questions. It would be better if each lecture were shorter and followed by a short discussion.

Overall, I was very satisfied with the workshop, so I would certainly attend another CareTalk event, especially one on working with patients' families.

Thank you once again for a well-organised course. I look forward to hearing about future events.

Yours sincerely,

Vì sao ăn điểm: đúng giọng formal (không viết tắt, Dear Mr Lewis → Yours sincerely); góp ý lịch sự bằng It would be better if…; câu trả lời ý 3 có lý do. Khung này dùng cho mọi đề "feedback on a course / conference / hotel / service".
Dạng C · Thư mời (informal)

You are organising a small party to celebrate finishing your course. Write an email to your friend Ben, and:

  • invite him to the party and say when and where it is,
  • tell him what you are planning to do at the party, and
  • ask him to help you with something.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu C · informal… từ

Hi Ben,

How are things? I hope you're well. I'm writing to invite you to a small party to celebrate the end of my course. It will be held at my flat on Saturday 25 October at 7 p.m.

We're planning to have a barbecue in the garden, so there will be plenty of food and cold drinks. Later on, we'll play some board games and, if the weather is good, watch a film outside. It's going to be very relaxed, so just wear something comfortable.

There's one thing I'd like to ask you. Could you bring your speaker and help me choose the music? You have much better taste than I do!

Let me know if you can come. It would be great to see you there.

Best wishes,

Vì sao ăn điểm: thời gian – địa điểm rõ (at … on … at …); kế hoạch dùng be going to / will; lời nhờ lịch sự Could you…?; câu điều kiện loại 1 (if the weather is good).
Dạng D · Xin lỗi + đề nghị khác (informal)

Your English friend, Jenny, is planning to visit you next month, but you cannot meet her on the dates she suggested. Write an email to Jenny, and:

  • apologise for not being able to meet her,
  • explain the reason, and
  • suggest a different time to meet.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu D · informal… từ

Dear Jenny,

Thanks for your message. It's lovely to hear that you're coming to Vietnam next month.

I'm really sorry, but I'm afraid I won't be able to meet you on 12–14 May, as you suggested. The reason is that I have my final exams that week, and I need to spend every free moment studying. I feel terrible about this, because I know you've already planned your trip.

However, how about meeting on the following weekend, 19–20 May? By then my exams will be over, and I'll be completely free. We could spend Saturday exploring the old quarter and take a boat trip on Sunday. If that doesn't suit you, just tell me which days work best and I'll arrange my time.

Once again, I'm sorry for the change of plan. I look forward to seeing you soon.

Love,

Vì sao ăn điểm: xin lỗi – lý do – đề nghị mới đi liền mạch; có tương lai hoàn thành (will be over) và điều kiện; kết xin lỗi lần nữa – rất "người thật".
Dạng E · Hỏi thông tin (formal)

You saw an advertisement for a summer English course at Bright Language School in Manchester. Write an email to the school, and:

  • say which course you are interested in and ask when it starts and how much it costs,
  • ask about accommodation, and
  • ask whether you will receive a certificate at the end.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu E · formal… từ

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to ask for some information about the four-week summer English course advertised on your website.

First of all, I would like to know when the course starts and how much the fee is. Could you also tell me whether the price includes course books and materials?

Secondly, I would be grateful if you could give me some details about accommodation. Is it possible to stay with a local family, and if so, how much does it cost per week?

Finally, I would also like to ask whether students receive a certificate at the end of the course, as I need one for my job application.

I look forward to your reply. Thank you in advance for your help.

Yours faithfully,

Vì sao ăn điểm: 3 câu hỏi gián tiếp lịch sự (I would like to know when… / whether…) – đúng ngữ pháp B2; Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully đi thành cặp.
Dạng F · Phàn nàn – yêu cầu (formal)

You recently bought a laptop from an online shop, but there were several problems. Write an email to the customer service department, and:

  • say what you bought and when,
  • explain the problems, and
  • say what you want the shop to do.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu F · formal… từ

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about a laptop (order number 4521) that I bought from your online shop on 3 March.

Unfortunately, there were several problems with the order. First, the laptop arrived five days later than promised. In addition, when I opened the box, I found that the screen was scratched and the charger was missing. As a result, I have not been able to use the computer for my work.

I would therefore like to request a replacement or a full refund. I would also be grateful if you could explain why the order was delayed.

I hope this matter can be resolved as soon as possible. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,

Vì sao ăn điểm: phàn nàn nhưng lịch sự, có chi tiết (số đơn, ngày); kết quả nêu bằng As a result; yêu cầu rõ (replacement / refund).
Dạng G · Cảm ơn (informal)

Last month you stayed with your friend Lisa's family in England. Write an email to Lisa, and:

  • thank her and her family for the stay,
  • say what you enjoyed most, and
  • invite her to visit you.

Write at least 120 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Bài mẫu G · informal… từ

Dear Lisa,

I'm writing to thank you and your family for letting me stay with you last month. It was so kind of you to look after me so well.

What I enjoyed most was the weekend trip to the Lake District. The views were amazing, and I still laugh when I remember how we got lost on the way back! I also loved your mum's cooking – please tell her that her apple pie was the best I've ever had.

To show my appreciation, I'd love you to come and visit me next summer. You could stay with my family for a week, and I'll take you to the beach and to the night market. I'm sure you'd enjoy it.

Task 1 (10 marks)

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have received an email from your friend Sophie, who is going to spend two weeks in your country in July. Read part of her email:

“I'd love to try some real Vietnamese food while I'm there! Which dishes should I try first? Is there a cooking class you could recommend? And what would be a good gift to bring for your family?”
Best,
Sophie

Write an email of reply to Sophie, and:

  • suggest two dishes she should try,
  • recommend a cooking class or a place to learn cooking, and
  • suggest a gift for your family.

Write about 120–150 words. Do NOT include your name or any addresses.

Task 2 (20 marks)

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Read the following topic:

Nowadays, many people do very little physical exercise. What problems can this cause? How can these problems be solved?

Write an essay to express your point of view. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.

Nộp cả bài = chấm cả 2 task và cộng điểm /30.
Bài mẫu Đề 1 · Task 1… từ

Dear Sophie,

Thanks for your email. I'm so glad you want to try real Vietnamese food while you're here – you're going to love it!

First of all, you should try pho, our famous noodle soup, and bun cha, which is grilled pork with rice noodles and fresh herbs. Both are cheap and sold everywhere, but the best places are the small family restaurants near the old market.

As for a cooking class, I'd recommend the Hanoi Kitchen Class in the city centre. It lasts three hours, includes a trip to the market, and the teacher speaks very good English. I can book a place for you if you like.

Finally, don't worry too much about a gift. My parents would be delighted with some tea or chocolate from your country – it's the thought that counts!

Let me know your flight details and I'll meet you at the airport.

Best wishes,

Bài mẫu Đề 1 · Task 2 (Problem – Solution)… từ

Nowadays, many people, especially those who work in offices, do very little physical exercise. This lifestyle causes several serious problems, but there are also effective ways to deal with them.

The most obvious problem is poor physical health. When people sit all day and rarely move, they tend to put on weight, and this can lead to conditions such as heart disease, high blood pressure and diabetes. Another problem is mental health. Because exercise helps the body release stress, people who never exercise often feel tired, anxious or unable to concentrate at work. In Vietnam, for example, many young office workers now complain of back pain and sleep problems that were once seen only in older people. Over time, these problems reduce productivity and increase the cost of healthcare for the whole country.

Fortunately, there are several solutions. First of all, individuals should build small amounts of exercise into their daily routine, such as walking to work, using the stairs instead of the lift or doing a short workout at home. Secondly, employers could encourage staff to be more active by organising sports clubs or allowing short exercise breaks during the day. Finally, local governments need to provide more parks, cycle paths and public sports facilities, so that exercise becomes easy and free for everyone. Simple changes like these cost little but can make a real difference.

In conclusion, a lack of exercise damages both physical and mental health. However, if individuals, employers and governments work together, these problems can be greatly reduced, and people will enjoy healthier and happier lives.

Task 2 (20 marks)

Some people believe that hospitals and clinics should use more technology, such as online appointments and video consultations, instead of face-to-face visits. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this development?

Write an essay to express your point of view. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.

Bài mẫu Đề 2 · Task 1 (formal)… từ

Dear Ms Wilson,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to give you some feedback on my recent stay at the Green Park Hotel.

What I liked most was the location and the staff. The hotel is only five minutes from the conference centre, and the receptionists were friendly and helpful, especially when my flight was delayed and I arrived late at night.

However, one thing that could be improved is the breakfast. The room was often crowded, and there were very few healthy choices such as fruit or yoghurt. It would be better if breakfast were served for a longer period and included more fresh options.

Overall, I enjoyed my stay very much, so I would certainly choose the Green Park Hotel again on my next work trip, and I will recommend it to my colleagues.

Thank you once again for your hospitality.

Yours sincerely,

Bài mẫu Đề 2 · Task 2 (Adv – Disadv)… từ

In recent years, many hospitals and clinics have started to use technology such as online appointment systems and video consultations instead of traditional face-to-face visits. This development has both advantages and disadvantages, which will be discussed in this essay.

The main advantage is convenience. Patients can book an appointment or talk to a doctor from home, without travelling long distances or waiting for hours in a crowded clinic. This is particularly helpful for elderly people, busy workers and those who live in rural areas. Another benefit is that technology saves time and money for hospitals: doctors can see more patients each day, and simple problems such as a cold or a repeat prescription can be dealt with in a few minutes online. In addition, digital records make it easier for doctors to follow a patient's history over time.

However, there are also some drawbacks. The biggest disadvantage is that a video call cannot replace a physical examination. Doctors cannot listen to a patient's chest or feel for a swelling through a screen, so serious conditions may be missed. In addition, not everyone has a good internet connection or the skills to use these systems, which means that older or poorer patients may receive worse care than others. Finally, some patients simply feel more comfortable when they can talk to a doctor in person.

In conclusion, online appointments and video consultations make healthcare faster and more convenient, but they cannot fully replace face-to-face visits. On balance, I believe that technology should be used alongside, rather than instead of, traditional care, so that all patients receive safe and equal treatment.

Task 1 (10 marks)

You have received an email from your former English teacher, Mrs Green, who is writing a report about what her past students are doing now. Read part of her email:

Task 2 (20 marks)

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Read the following topic:

Some people think that universities should require all students to do voluntary work in their local community. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Write an essay to express your point of view. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.

Bài mẫu Đề 3 · Task 1 (semi-formal)… từ

Dear Mrs Green,

Thank you for your email. It was lovely to hear from you again, and I am happy to help with your report.

At the moment, I am working as a nurse at a district hospital while studying for a master's degree in public health. My job involves looking after patients in the emergency department, which is busy but never boring.

What I enjoy most is working with people. Every day I meet patients from different backgrounds, and helping them feel calm and safe gives me a real sense of satisfaction. I also enjoy the teamwork, because doctors and nurses have to trust each other completely.

Your English classes have helped me a great deal. The presentation practice gave me the confidence to speak to foreign doctors at work, and the writing lessons made it much easier for me to read medical articles in English.

Thank you once again for everything you taught us. I hope we can meet at the school reunion next year.

Best regards,

Bài mẫu Đề 3 · Task 2 (Opinion – disagree)… từ

It is sometimes suggested that universities should require all students to do voluntary work in their local community. While I understand the reasons behind this idea, I disagree that it should be compulsory.

The first reason is that voluntary work, by definition, should be a free choice. If students are forced to take part, many will do the minimum amount of work without real interest, and the local community will gain very little. For example, a student who is made to clean a park every Sunday is unlikely to work as carefully as someone who chose to do it. In other words, the value of voluntary work comes from the willingness of the volunteer.

Another important reason is that many students already have heavy workloads. Medical and engineering students, in particular, spend long hours in lectures, laboratories and hospitals, and some also have part-time jobs to support their families. Adding compulsory community service would increase their stress and could even harm their academic results. For example, a final-year medical student who works in a hospital ward for forty hours a week simply has no time for an extra compulsory activity.

Some people may argue that compulsory service teaches responsibility and social skills. However, universities can achieve the same goal by offering attractive optional programmes, giving credits for voluntary projects, or encouraging student clubs to organise charity events. These approaches develop responsibility without forcing anyone.

In conclusion, although voluntary work brings clear benefits to both students and communities, I believe that it should remain voluntary. Universities should encourage and reward it, rather than make it a requirement.
